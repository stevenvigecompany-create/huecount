Privacy Policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

Huecount saves your progress and settings only on your device (browser storage). We don't have accounts, don't collect your name or email in the game, and don't use advertising or tracking cookies. If Huecount Plus goes on sale, payment is handled by Stripe, and we keep only what is needed to restore your unlock. Questions or deletion requests: hello@unfinishedop.com.

Huecount includes flashing colors. If you have photosensitive epilepsy, use the Reduce flashes setting or don't play.
